How the matching works

  1. We read each program's own rules

    Every program is researched from its own published material — who it serves, what it pays, the income limit it actually uses. One program, one source; we never judge a program by another program's figures.

  2. We compare them to your answers

    Your county, household size, income and situation are checked against those rules. You see which requirements you meet and which ones we could not confirm — not a score.

  3. Anything unconfirmed stays on the list

    A missing figure or an unanswered question never rules a program out. It is shown with the open question named, because the program is the only one who can settle it.

This tool is being tested and is not official. It gives general information, not a decision about your eligibility. Program details and income figures are estimates — only the programs themselves can tell you if you qualify.
